# Informative document-review profile

This profile demonstrates a non-software, human-in-the-loop harness that reviews
a bounded document set.

Additional concerns usually include:

- supported document formats and extraction fidelity;
- source authority, freshness, and citation locations;
- confidential or regulated data handling;
- explicit separation of findings, inference, contradiction, and uncertainty;
- human authority for final decisions;
- deterministic coverage and citation checks;
- adversarial or conflicting documents; and
- reassessment when the question, document set, extraction method, or decision
  context changes.

See [`../examples/document-review/hdp.yaml`](../examples/document-review/hdp.yaml).
